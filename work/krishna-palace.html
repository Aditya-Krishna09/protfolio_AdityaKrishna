01 / OVERVIEW

The context behind the work.

A hands-on digital business and marketing engagement for Krishna Palace in Palojori, Deoghar. The work focused on presenting the property clearly online and creating useful digital assets for its hospitality and event offering.

Krishna Palace is a hospitality business with rooms and event facilities. The digital work focused on making the property easier to understand online and giving prospective guests a clearer route to explore the offering and make enquiries.

My role: Digital Business & Marketing.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Start with the real problem.

Hospitality decisions are often made from a combination of property information, photographs, amenities, event capacity, and easy enquiry pathways. That information needs to be organised and communicated consistently across digital touchpoints.
