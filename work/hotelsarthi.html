01 / OVERVIEW

The context behind the work.

HotelSarthi is a hotel-management SaaS product shaped by conversations with hotel operators and the day-to-day realities of running a property. My role spans product direction and the customer journey—from discovering needs to onboarding hotels and improving the product.

The opportunity was to build around practical hotel workflows, not technology for its own sake. Hotel teams need essential property information and everyday operational tasks to feel clear, accessible, and manageable. HotelSarthi is being developed with that operating context in mind.

My role: Founder · Product & Business Development.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Start with the real problem.

Hotel operations involve multiple recurring workflows—front desk coordination, billing, guest information, and business visibility. A useful product must fit the way independent hotels actually work and remain understandable to the people using it.
