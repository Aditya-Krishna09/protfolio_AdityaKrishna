01 / OVERVIEW

The context behind the work.

A climate-finance strategy submission for Parivartan ’26 at TISS Mumbai. The proposal focused on a practical conversion gap: preparing projects, pricing risks, reaching financial close, commissioning assets, and recycling capital after performance is proven.

The presentation argued that India’s climate-finance challenge is not only the amount of capital announced, but also the ability to convert support into bankable, commissioned assets. The proposed Climate Capital Conversion Window (CCB 2.0) organised the process around four gates: prepare, de-risk, commission, and recycle.

My role: Team Lead · Team ak.adityakrishna09.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Start with the real problem.

A project pipeline can have public support and still face gaps in tariff discipline, financial close, delivery, and operating proof. The proposal therefore focused on defined risks and milestone-based capital release rather than open-ended guarantees.

03 / APPROACH

How the work came together.

01

Prepare

Complete project data rooms covering land, grid, permits, offtake, EPC, and OEM warranties.

02

Underwrite

Reprice bids and test cash-flow coverage, augmentation reserves, and downside conditions.

03

Build & commission

Release funds against milestones and independently verify performance and safety.

04

Recycle capital

Refinance operating assets after 12–24 months of verified operating evidence.
