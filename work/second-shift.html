01 / OVERVIEW

The context behind the work.

An individual case submission for a specialist marketplace that connects experienced financial customer-operations professionals with AI and FinTech product teams. The concept converts domain expertise into verified evaluation work rather than treating professionals as a generic freelancer pool.

The proposal focused on experienced financial-services customer-operations and escalation professionals. Their domain context—customer situations, exceptions, escalation rules, and service quality—could be valuable in evaluating AI systems built for financial customer service.

My role: Individual participant.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Start with the real problem.

Routine work is increasingly exposed to automation, while many AI systems still need human judgement to evaluate ambiguous cases, edge conditions, escalation behaviour, and service quality. The concept aimed to create a credible bridge from experience to paid AI evaluation work.

03 / APPROACH

How the work came together.

01

Map experience

Document real roles, tasks, and domain experience.

02

Verify capability

03

04

Score AI outputs, create edge cases, identify escalation issues, and produce structured quality reports.
