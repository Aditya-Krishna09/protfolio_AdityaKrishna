01 / OVERVIEW

The context behind the work.

A team-led growth strategy proposal developed for Boardroom Brawlz at Drishti 2026. The challenge was to design a route toward 10,000 litres per day while managing local demand, channel sequencing, freshness constraints, cold-chain economics, and launch execution.

The case framed a 3.23× scale-up as a demand–supply coordination problem. The case baseline was approximately 3,100 L/day, with a proposed target of 10,000 L/day across five cities. A larger distribution footprint would only create value if demand velocity and remaining shelf life were managed together.

My role: Team Lead · ScopeEdge.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Start with the real problem.

The product had a two-day chilled shelf life at 3–4°C. The team needed to sequence cities and channels without releasing inventory ahead of proven local demand, while protecting contribution after logistics and wastage.

03 / APPROACH

How the work came together.
